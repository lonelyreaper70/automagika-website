01 — WHO WE ARE

Small team.
Big engineering ambitions.

AutoMagika Industries is a student-led initiative exploring the intersection of aerospace, robotics, intelligent systems and experimental design.

We believe a concept becomes meaningful when it is challenged, tested, improved and built. Our work ranges from physical prototypes to software concepts and research-driven projects.

Every project is another opportunity to learn something new, solve a difficult problem and push our capabilities further.

THE PROJECT

Inspired by stealth.
Built to learn.

DRACO-F22 is AutoMagika's experimental RC aircraft platform. Its development explores airframe design, onboard electronics, servo actuation, propulsion integration and flight-control challenges.

The schematic above is an original F-22-inspired illustration, not a dimensionally accurate Lockheed Martin blueprint or a representation of the exact DRACO airframe.

PROPULSION & AEROSPACE UNDER RESEARCH

Jet Technology

An exploratory research direction focused on understanding jet propulsion principles, aircraft performance and the engineering challenges of high-speed flight.

Propulsion fundamentals Flight simulation Thermal & material constraints High-speed aerodynamics
